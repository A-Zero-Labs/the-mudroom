# Security

The Mudroom is shared for inspiration. It is an example of how a personal inbox assistant
can work, and it is not supported software: there are no security updates, no fixes on a
schedule and no promise of an answer.

Your copy is yours. You can change anything in it, and you are responsible for what you do
with it: which accounts you connect, what you let it read, and what you approve in your
name. Read the code before you give it access to your mail or chats. It comes without
warranty, as the [licence](LICENSE) says.

If you find a weakness, please do not post the details in a public issue. Use the
*Report a vulnerability* button on the repository's Security tab, so others are not put at
risk while you tell us. We read what comes in when we can, and we may or may not change the
project because of it.
